# Transfer and Comfort Tips

## Fast interchanges

- Ameerpet (Blue/Red): stay on the same concourse, 3–4 minutes.
- MG Bus Station (Blue/Green): same concourse, 2–3 minutes.
- Jubilee Bus Station (Red/Green): cross-platform style walk, 3 minutes.

## Avoiding long walks

- Begumpet to Parade is a 1.85 km walk; only use it when trains on the Red Line
  are suspended.
- Hyderabad Deccan to MG Bus Station is 950 m; a bus or metro alternative
  usually costs less time than walking in the rain.

## Comfort preferences

- Buses after 21:00 are quieter; metros are safer for late journeys because of
  station staff.
- Wheelchair users should prefer Ameerpet and MG Bus Station for transfers.
- If you are in a hurry, metro beats buses in peak hours even when the bus is
  cheaper.

## Luggage

- MMTS trains have more space for luggage than the metro during peak hours.
- Escalators at Miyapur and HITEC City can be busy at 09:00.
