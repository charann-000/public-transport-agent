# Station and Interchange Notes

## LB Nagar
Southern terminus of the Blue Line with a large bus terminal. Exit from the metro
is on the west side; auto-rickshaws and city buses are available outside.
Interchanges: Metro Blue Line, city buses.

## HITEC City
Tech-corridor station on the Blue Line. Peak-hour crowding is common between
08:30 and 10:30. The exit towards Madhapur is closer to most office campuses.

## Ameerpet
Major interchange between the Blue Line and the Red Line, and a hub for city
buses. Expect 3 to 4 minutes to change lines inside the fare gate area. A
750 metre walking connection to Begumpet station is available outside the gates.

## MG Bus Station (MGBS)
Interchange for the Blue Line, the Green Line and the MMTS corridor via a short
walk to Hyderabad Deccan. The bus terminal handles outstation and city buses.

## Begumpet
Served by the Red Line and MMTS trains. The walking path to Parade takes about
20 minutes; use it only when trains are disrupted.

## Secunderabad
Railway junction with the Red Line metro. Platform changes inside the railway
station can take time with luggage. A 1.6 km walk to Jubilee Bus Station is
signposted.

## Jubilee Bus Station (JBS)
Terminus of the Red Line and start of the Green Line towards MG Bus Station.

## Hyderabad Deccan (Nampally)
MMTS station close to the Nampally metro station; a 950 metre footpath connects
it to MG Bus Station.

## Nagole, Uppal, Dilsukhnagar
Eastern corridor stations. Nagole and LB Nagar are one metro stop apart.
