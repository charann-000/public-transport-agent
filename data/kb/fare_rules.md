# Fare Rules (Hyderabad sample network)

Fares are charged **per boarding** of a paid service. Walking is always free.

## Metro (Blue, Red, Green lines)

Distance tiers based on the number of stations travelled in a single ride:

| Stations travelled | Fare |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 3 | ₹10 |
| 4 to 6 | ₹20 |
| 7 to 9 | ₹30 |
| 10 or more | ₹40 |

A transfer to another metro line is a new boarding, so the fare for each ride is
calculated separately. Example: LB Nagar to HITEC City on the Blue Line travels
9 stations, so the fare is ₹30.

## MMTS suburban trains

| Stations travelled | Fare |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 4 | ₹10 |
| 5 or more | ₹15 |

## TSRTC city buses

| Stations travelled | Fare |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 4 | ₹12 |
| 5 to 8 | ₹18 |
| 9 or more | ₹24 |

## Concessions and passes

- Daily and monthly bus passes are accepted on buses; the planner does not apply
  pass discounts in the MVP.
- Senior citizen and student concessions apply to metro and MMTS fares when a
  valid photo ID is shown; the planner uses standard adult fares.

## Budget tips

- Bus travel is the cheapest option on almost every corridor.
- Metro is fastest during peak hours because it is grade-separated.
- Combining one metro boarding with a short bus leg usually stays under ₹50 for
  cross-city trips.
