# Operating Hours and Frequency

## Metro (Blue, Red, Green lines)

- First train: 06:30 from the terminal stations.
- Last train: 22:30 (entry closes at 22:15).
- Peak hours: 08:00–10:30 and 17:00–20:00.
- Frequency: every 5–6 minutes in peak hours, every 8–10 minutes off-peak.

## MMTS suburban trains

- First train: 05:30, last train: 22:45 from Secunderabad.
- Frequency: roughly every 15–20 minutes on the Deccan corridor.
- Trains can be crowded between 08:00 and 10:00.

## City buses

- Most routes run 06:00–22:30.
- Frequency varies from 5 minutes on trunk routes to 15 minutes on others.
- Night services are limited after 22:00.

## Planner behaviour

- Journeys must arrive before the last service of the day.
- Departures before the first train are suggested with buses instead.
- During festivals and Sundays, frequencies may be reduced.
