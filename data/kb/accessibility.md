# Accessibility Information

## Step-free access

- Metro stations Ameerpet, HITEC City, LB Nagar, Miyapur and Secunderabad have
  lifts from the concourse to the platforms.
- Escalators are available at all Blue and Red Line interchange stations.
- MMTS platforms at Begumpet and Secunderabad have ramps at selected entries,
  but some platforms require stairs.

## Walking and transfers

- Prefer interchanges with lift access (Ameerpet, MG Bus Station) if you are
  carrying luggage or using a wheelchair.
- The 750 m Ameerpet–Begumpet footpath has two signal crossings.
- The 1.6 km Secunderabad–Jubilee Bus Station walk is unshaded; avoid it in
  peak afternoon heat.
- Wheelchair users should allow extra time for bus ramps, which are available on
  about half the city fleet.

## Assistance

- Metro staff at the customer service desk can help with wheelchair boarding.
- Buses with low-floor entry are marked at the front.

## Planning tip

- Set a low maximum walking distance in the planner to receive routes with
  lifts and shorter transfers.
